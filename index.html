What This Is

A field reference tool for building diagnostic intuition — not a certified diagnosis. It reasons only from what you type in; it can't see the equipment, verify wiring, or replace a hands-on inspection. Treat every result as where to start looking, not a final call.

⚠ Electrical Safety

Run capacitors can hold a lethal charge even with the system fully powered off. Always properly discharge a capacitor before touching its terminals, and follow lockout/tagout procedures. Never work on energized components without the training and PPE your job requires.

⚠ Refrigerant Safety

Follow EPA Section 608 refrigerant handling requirements at all times. Never vent refrigerant to atmosphere.

Before You Charge Anything

Always verify target superheat/subcooling against the unit's own data plate, access panel sticker, or install manual when available, and confirm any finding here against your own judgment and training before acting on it.
