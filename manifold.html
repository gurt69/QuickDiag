No system catches every variable, so treat this as where to start looking, not a final call. #1 is the most likely place to check first.

Built around the readings a Job Link–style probe kit already gives you — this tool doesn't calculate superheat, subcooling, or saturation temps itself, it reasons from what your probes report. Reference tool for building diagnostic intuition, not a substitute for manufacturer charging charts or your own field verification. Always confirm target superheat/subcooling against the unit's data plate or install manual, and follow EPA 608 refrigerant handling practices.
